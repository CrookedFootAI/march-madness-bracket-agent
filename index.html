NIL SPENDING & BRACKET ROI
How to use NIL data when picking your bracket · Estimated 2025-26 roster costs · Source: The Athletic / On3 / nil-ncaa.com
HOW NIL AFFECTS YOUR BRACKET: NIL spend buys roster depth — critical for a 6-game run when foul trouble, fatigue, and injuries compound. But money doesn't buy cohesion, coaching, or clutch DNA. The sweet spot is $10–15M with an experienced coach. Below that you lack depth. Above $15M you often have a transfer-heavy roster with zero chemistry.
